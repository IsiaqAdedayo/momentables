### LinkedIn

## Engagement playbook (do this daily)

1. **20 minutes before posting** — comment thoughtfully on 4–5 posts from museum accounts, digital artists, and art-tech founders. Warm the algorithm with people who actually buy.
2. **Post, then reply to every comment within 60 minutes** with a question back, not "thanks!"
3. **Cross-post with a twist**, not copy-paste: LinkedIn = the argument + the lesson. X = the one line that lands.
4. **Quote-tweet your Day 2 (the rebuild) post on Day 4**: "This got the most replies. So here's what we're actually building toward 👇"
5. **Save the best DMs** (anonymised) for week 2 — real curator and artist questions become next week's posts.
6. **Never claim a sale, an artist, or a metric you can't back.** The named drops above are demo content — swap in real works before Day 3 goes out, or mark the section clearly as illustrative.
